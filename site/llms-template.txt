# openxet

> openxet is the open-source (Apache-2.0) server and client for the Xet protocol, Hugging Face's chunk-deduplicated storage for large, versioned files. The official clients (hf, huggingface_hub, hf_xet, git-xet, git-lfs, huggingface.js) work against it unmodified. It runs on storage and identity systems the operator already owns, and will be offered as a hosted service at openxet.com. Status: in active development before 1.0; the changelog says what works today.

openxet is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Hugging Face.
